Easy-Dissolve Comparison

Include this footage when it fits your concept, or recreate it:

  1. Prep the water: two clear glasses, ~8 oz water each at 60°C / 140°F — no ice water, no ice.
  2. Open the packets: snip one corner of each packet with scissors.
  3. Add gradually: pour each powder slowly and continuously into its glass — don't dump it all at once.
  4. Mix & rest: swirl or stir each glass; let both sit for ~30 seconds.
  5. Show the result: film both glasses side by side once the water is clear.
